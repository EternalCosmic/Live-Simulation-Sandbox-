The Kármán Line (100 km)

At 100km, the atmosphere becomes too thin to provide aerodynamic lift. To generate lift, an airplane would have to fly faster than orbital velocity!

Air Density: < 0.001% Sea Level
Speed Requirement: > 7.8 km/s

Part 1: Leaving Earth (Action & Reaction)

Newton’s Third Law: Momentum conservation drives rocket propulsion in vacuum.

Exhaust Vector: Downward Thrust Vector: Upward
$\mathbf{F}_{thrust} = \dot{m} \cdot v_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e$

Rockets do not push against air. Throwing mass backward pushes the vehicle forward.

The Tyranny of the Rocket Equation

Tsiolkovsky’s Equation dictates exponential fuel demands.

$\Delta v = v_e \ln \left( \frac{m_0}{m_f} \right)$

To push fuel, you need more fuel to push that fuel.
